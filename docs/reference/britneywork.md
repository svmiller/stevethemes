# The Civilian Unemployment Rate, 2013-2018

This is a simple data set on the monthly civilian unemployment rate in
the United States from 2013 to 2018. This is useful for illustrating the
powerful effect Britney Spears had on the job prospects of American
workers.

## Usage

``` r
britneywork
```

## Format

A data frame with 63 rows and two columns

- `date`:

  a date

- `value`:

  the civilian unemployment rate

## Details

Data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, by way of the Federal
Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Inspired by a claim Donald Trump made early
into his first presidential tenure about how he single-handedly
decreased the unemployment rate in the United States. He didn't do that;
Britney Spears did.
